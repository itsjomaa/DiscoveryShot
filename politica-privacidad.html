Last updated: August 2026

1. DATA CONTROLLER

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR 2016/679) and the Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPD-GDD), you are informed that any personal data provided through this website will be processed by:

  • Controller: Discovery Shot (Charles Sarmiento)
  • Location: Barcelona, Spain
  • Website: www.discoveryshot.one
  • Contact Email: discoveryshot23@gmail.com

2. DATA WE COLLECT AND PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

Discovery Shot collects and processes personal data solely for legitimate and transparent purposes:

A. Contact and Inquiry Information

When you contact us via email or social media to inquire about photography sessions or video production services, your information (name, email address, phone number, project details) is processed exclusively to handle your request and provide a tailored proposal.

B. Browsing Data & Analytics (Google Analytics)

Through analytical cookies (only if you have granted express prior consent), we collect anonymized statistical data such as browser type, pages visited, session duration, and approximate geographic location to improve website performance and user experience.

3. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING

The processing of your data is based on the following legal grounds under Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • Consent of the Data Subject (Art. 6.1.a GDPR): For the deployment of analytical cookies and answering inquiries initiated voluntarily by the user.
  • Performance of a Contract or Pre-contractual Measures (Art. 6.1.b GDPR): In the event of booking and executing photography or audiovisual services.
  • Legitimate Interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR): To ensure website security, performance, and fraud prevention.

4. DATA RETENTION PERIOD

Personal data will be stored only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected:

  • General inquiries: Stored for the duration needed to respond to and manage your request, and deleted thereafter unless a business contract ensues.
  • Client data: Retained for the duration of the contracted service and subsequently for the statutory limitation periods required under applicable civil and tax regulations.
  • Google Analytics data: Stored in accordance with standard GA4 retention settings (up to 14 months).

5. DATA RECIPIENTS AND INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Discovery Shot will not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except where legally required.

For anonymous web analytics, we use Google Analytics (provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). Google participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, ensuring an adequate level of data protection in compliance with European standards.

6. YOUR RIGHTS AS A DATA SUBJECT (GDPR)

Under the GDPR and Spanish data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation on whether your personal data is being processed and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your personal data when no longer necessary.
  • Object: Object to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests.
  • Restriction of Processing: Request the temporary restriction of data processing under certain circumstances.
  • Data Portability: Receive your personal data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, please send an email to contacto@discoveryshot.one clearly stating your request along with proof of identity.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, in Spain the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.
