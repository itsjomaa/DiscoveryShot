COOKIE POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your browser or device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit the page. Cookies allow the website to remember information about your visit (such as your consent choices or anonymous visitor metrics) to offer you a smoother, more secure browsing experience.

2. TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

On Discovery Shot, we utilize the following categories of cookies:

A. Necessary / Technical Cookies (Exempt from consent)

These cookies are strictly necessary for the website to function properly. For example, remembering your preference regarding cookie consent via browser local storage (localStorage) so the banner is not shown repeatedly.

B. Analytical / Performance Cookies (Require prior consent)

These cookies, processed by third parties (Google Analytics), allow us to measure traffic, understand how visitors interact with our photography portfolio, and improve site content. They are activated only after you grant consent.
